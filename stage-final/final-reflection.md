# Group Reflection 
**Group 32: Ayesha Qadir, Jatinder Sahota, Kriza Cyrene del Moro**

We all feel the group communicated very well, particularly near the end. In our last reflection, we discussed more regularly scheduled meetings and checking group messages more consistently. Both of those have been going very well. We tried our best to assign tasks in such a way that everyone could work independently as much as possible, and everyone was quick to respond when discussion was needed. Our meetings were brief but productive, and helped speed up task delegation and decision making. These meetings also helped us set hard and soft deadlines as they functioned as sort of check-ins on how the final stage was coming along.

We divided the final stage work into 3 broad parts: frontend, backend, and report writing. Each member was assigned one part, and all felt that the work was fairly evenly split. Each member was able to consult the rest of the team on issues and questions that arose, so in that way, everyone stayed up to date on each component of the project; thus, all members were able to work efficiently on their own but were never disconnected from the group. Additionally, everyone was able to complete work at their own pace/when it was convenient for them, with the soft and hard deadlines as guidance. Regular check-ins with the group meant no one fell behind, and everyone completed their work on time.

From the beginning, our group had a very reasonable and modest scope for the project. None of us knew much about F1 going in (we chose it because we felt it would be interesting to pick a dataset none of us was familiar with), and it helped us avoid getting stuck in the small details or bite off more than we could collectively chew. 

We did get rid of a few entities at the very begininning because we felt they would be hard to model properly with the available data and would not have many interesting queries. In hindsight, this allowed us to focus our efforts on the more important and interesting data. We also had some changes in the plans for our UI, though this was more due to the business of the end of the semester forcing us to make many quick decisions. It did not decrease the scope necessarily, but it did change from our original vision for the project. Still, we ended with something we are all quite happy with.
