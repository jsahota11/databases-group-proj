# Group Reflection 
**Group 32: Ayesha Qadir, Jatinder Sahota, Kriza Cyrene del Moro**

- How well is the group communicating? How can communication be improved?
- Has the work been divided evenly among team members?
- Is everyone completing their assigned work on time? If not, what adjustments will be made moving forward to make the project successful?

---
Our communication has been going reasonably well, but there are a few shortcomings. The main one is that our meetings have been a bit sparse; in particular, they have been less frequent than we originally intended. This is due in large part to our changing schedules, making regularly scheduled meetings more difficult than initially anticipated. That, in turn, means that we have to rely more heavily on messaging in our Discord server to communicate. 

We intend to improve communication by committing to checking and engaging with messages from the group more quickly, so that any matters that need to be discussed can be resolved as soon as possible. We have also scheduled three online meetings for the last two weeks with everyone's schedules taken into account, so that hopefully they will not need to be rescheduled or missed. These regular meetings will also keep us all on the same page, even if we aren't keeping up with messages to the higher degree that we hope to. 

As stated in the last reflection, the work still feels relatively evenly divided. None of us feel like we are doing too much or that the workload is not sustainable. There isn't much more we can strive for here, so we will try to keep this trend going.

We would like to get tasks completed earlier than they have been. There is the possibility of things being missed or work that is less than satisfactory being submitted when things get done right at the deadline. Ideally, we will have a few days during this last stretch to perfect our final submission and get all components finished to a level that is satisfactory for the whole group. What remains to be done in the project is mainly putting all the pieces together, so we are hopeful that with a little bit of commitment, this will be doable!
