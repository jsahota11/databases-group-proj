# Group Reflection 
**Group 32: Ayesha Qadir, Jatinder Sahota, Kriza Cyrene del Moro**

We all believe that we have been doing well in terms of communication, as we regularly schedule mandatory weekly in-person meetings. In the case that these meetings are not enough, and further discussion is necessary, we schedule virtual meetings that we all have been able to attend so far as well. These meetings have allowed us to be up-to-date with everything that has been going on, and everyone has acknowledged that each of us know what is going on.

Our in-person meetings allow us to discuss what we are individually working on, but we mostly use these meetings to discuss what we plan for the tasks ahead of us—such as assigning tasks, setting deadlines, dividing the work, and our schedules to ensure we are assigning deadlines appropriately. Outside these meetings, we also have a Discord server. We all collectively agreed to keep our notifications on, so we would not miss any urgent updates. Our server is organized by channels like: general discussion, deadlines and documents, advice, and meetings and tasks, in order to make it accessible and easy to navigate on top of organization purposes. We mainly use these channels to communicate our questions, concerns, and progress, and everyone has been good at responding; we always respond to each other within the day. Although, we believe that we can improve our communication by providing more detailed updates on how tasks are getting done by each member.

Work has been evenly divided among us. None of us feel that we are overwhelmed, and we willingly accommodate to each other if needed (i.e. when someone has schedule conflicts, double booking, etc.). This has allowed us to get things done on time or at least within the timeline the group agreed upon on. We have had to adjust some deadlines, but it has never been due to any individual’s delay; these schedule shifts are collectively agreed on by each member. Everyone has been really good at adjusting as a group. We actively help each other out with resources and better understanding of course material whenever needed. We plan to improve our workflow by adding internal deadlines in our Discord server to ensure everyone has an easier time keeping up with tasks—especially prior meetings.
